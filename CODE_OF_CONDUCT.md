# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels. Give actionable feedback, accept corrections in good faith, and focus discussion on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or insulting language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted private contact, and publishing another person's private information without consent.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and applying these standards. They may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and restrict participation when necessary. Reports should be handled privately and reviewed fairly.

This checkout does not yet identify a public repository or reporting contact. Before inviting public contributions, maintainers must configure and publish a private contact path for conduct reports.
